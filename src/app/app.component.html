Pokédex Help

  • DRAG & DROP: Drag Pokémon sprites directly from the main display or the right-side list into the 6 Pokéball slots at the bottom dock to build your team.
  • NEW TEAM: Click the + button on the left of the dock to create a new team.
  • CHANGE NAME: Click the current team's name (e.g. TEAM : Pro) in the dock to rename it.
  • NAVIGATE TEAMS: Use the Left (◄) and Right (►) arrow buttons on the dock to switch between your saved teams.
  • STATS BUTTON: Click the yellow STATS button in the main viewer to open a detailed Radar Chart and view abilities.
  • BST (Base Stat Total): The sum of a Pokémon's core stats (HP, Atk, Def, SpA, SpD, Spe). The dock shows your team's total BST!
